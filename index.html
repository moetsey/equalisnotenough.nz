The petition

Life imprisonment for raping a child, unless a judge finds that manifestly unjust. Exactly as murder is today.

The petition asks for life imprisonment as the maximum for sexual violation. Our written submission asks that where the victim is under 18 it be the presumed sentence unless a judge finds that manifestly unjust, exactly as murder is today.

It also asks Parliament to raise the maximums for sexual offences against children and for abuse material, with minimum prison terms and no home detention, and to require two-thirds of a sentence to be served.

The request, as lodged

That the House of Representatives legislate to make life imprisonment the maximum for sexual violation, raise maximums for sexual offences against children and for child sexual abuse material, set minimum prison terms with no home detention for that material, and require two-thirds to be served.
